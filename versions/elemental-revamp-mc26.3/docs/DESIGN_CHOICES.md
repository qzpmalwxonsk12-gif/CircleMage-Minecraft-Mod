# Additional choices made for this revamp

These fill gaps in the request and are adjustable balance decisions.

1. **A loadout rather than forgetting magic.** Learned spells stay learned. Switching T1 automatically replaces the previous T1; T2 has three equipped slots. The limits apply at later circles as well. The bypass command permanently removes only slot-count limits for that player.
2. **Circle-gated school tomes.** The same five tome types teach their school's eligible T1–T3 spells, choosing one unknown spell at random. A tome with no eligible new spell is retained. Higher-tier knowledge cannot be obtained early through a tome.
3. **Steam is a T3 recipe.** Fireball + Ice Volley knowledge unlocks a separate Steam tree entry. Requiring two equipped T1 spells would conflict with the requested one-slot limit. Its water sphere is radius 2; its final cloud is radius 6 (12 blocks across), with one second of formation plus 30 seconds of cloud life.
4. **Soothing Water doubles natural mana.** “Natural regen” is interpreted as the existing mod's passive mana flow. It does not double health regeneration or channeling gain. It prevents ignition/fire damage, Steam damage and Steam fog for 30 seconds.
5. **Stone Fist: 5 HP. Stone Dome: radius 3, six seconds.** These were unspecified. Ground eruptions have an eight-tick rise before damage; the dome retains its original occupants, excludes new entrants and blocks crossing projectiles. Spell geometry uses temporary entities, never temporary terrain blocks.
6. **Aiming and escape details.** New targeted spells use a 32-block terrain-limited ray and a 2-block tolerance. Look down more than 65° for self-Dome. Earth magic requires ground within six blocks below the cursor/target. Roots block Wind Escape. Rebirth refuses a lethal 3 HP payment and keeps beneficial status effects.
7. **Ice slow cap.** Each hit adds five percentage points for four seconds, capped at 30% to avoid permanent zero movement from repeated volleys. Independent ice/wind hits reset their own hit invulnerability window so all three requested hits can register.
8. **Guardian witnesses.** Nearby players within 32 blocks who can see the guardian share the epiphany. Ignition from the T1 fireball or another fire source triggers the guardian's Dispel. Its eight-second cooldown prevents repeated fire-dispel spam. Insight remains a five-percent roll per successful cast after witnessing, not per hit.
9. **Friendly fire and defenses.** Steam affects living occupants, including the caster. Soothing Water is the preparation counter; Rebirth/Dispel remove the appropriate new effects. Ordinary spell damage still respects applicable target defenses. Missile boosts are retained; explicitly requested new damage uses fixed base values.
10. **J and controls.** J opens the tree and conflicting J bindings are cleared on client startup. Other spell shortcuts begin unbound, except G for held Dispel. Circle, knowledge and equipped-state checks run on the server for every input route. The journal uses the original book art; new spells use restrained procedural meshes, vanilla textures, particles and sound.
11. **Preservation and later progression.** The existing nine-circle thresholds, mana infusion, crafting, equipment, NPCs/bosses, arrays, chants and Laws were retained. That includes the original ten-billion-mana ninth circle and later advancement gates. Old player transformations and unused combination spells remain internal code for future reuse, but the new player's tree is authoritative.
12. **Tower air cleanup.** The deployed NBT files omit exterior air while retaining air with structural material above and below its column, preserving rooms. Block palettes, non-air block records, entities and template dimensions are preserved. This removes 64,093 exterior air records across the four templates. It affects future placements and does not fill existing holes in saved worlds.

The base-cost table is in [PLAYER_GUIDE.md](PLAYER_GUIDE.md). This is an alpha balance pass; sustained survival play and multiplayer balancing remain to be done.
